walktok Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 25, 2026

walktok is a hobby iOS app that lets small groups of friends ("crews") race each other by daily step count, taunt each other in a shared feed, and play silly power-ups against each other. This document explains what data the app collects, what we do with it, and how to get it removed.

Who runs walktok

walktok is an independent personal project. It is not affiliated with any company. The developer can be reached at neilsgr8@gmail.com.

What data walktok collects

Profile information you provide

  • A display name you choose during onboarding.
  • A cartoon avatar (color and face) you pick from preset options.
  • A short "vibe" label and in-game cosmetic choices.

Crew and gameplay data

  • Which crew you create or join, and the crew's invite code.
  • In-game inventory, equipped cosmetics, and unlocks.
  • Power-up plays you make against other crew members.
  • Short trash-talk activity feed entries tied to gameplay events.

Step data from your device

  • With your permission (the iOS "Motion & Fitness" prompt), walktok reads your daily step count from the iOS pedometer and records it against your profile so your crew can see today's leaderboard.
  • walktok records per-day step totals and step delta events (with a source label like pedometer, manual, powerup, or reward). It does not read GPS, heart rate, sleep data, or any other HealthKit information.
  • You can revoke motion access at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Motion & Fitness. The app will then fall back to its offline demo mode.

What walktok does NOT collect

  • No precise or coarse location.
  • No contacts, photos, microphone, or camera access.
  • No advertising identifiers, no ad tracking, no analytics SDKs.
  • No payment information — the app has no paid features.
  • No HealthKit data beyond the pedometer step counter described above.

How your data is used

  • To show your crew's leaderboard and shared activity feed.
  • To run gameplay (power-ups, unlocks, cosmetics).
  • To persist your profile between sessions on the same device.

Your data is not used for advertising, profiling, or sold to anyone.

Where your data is stored

Profile, crew, step, and feed data is stored in a Supabase project (PostgreSQL hosted by Supabase, Inc.) operated by the developer. Supabase processes this data on the developer's behalf and is bound by Supabase's own privacy and security commitments. See supabase.com/privacy.

A copy of your profile and recent gameplay state is also cached locally on your iPhone using the iOS app's storage so the app can start quickly and work briefly offline.

Who can see your data

  • Your crew: other members of any crew you join can see your display name, avatar, daily step totals, and feed activity inside that crew.
  • The developer: as the operator of the Supabase backend, the developer has technical access to stored data for debugging, abuse handling, and improving the app.
  • Supabase, Inc.: as the database host.
  • Apple: while walktok is distributed via TestFlight, Apple receives crash logs and basic install metadata under Apple's privacy policy.

walktok does not sell or rent your data and does not share it with any other third parties.

Data retention

Profile, crew, and step data is retained until you ask for it to be deleted or the walktok backend is shut down. Because walktok is a hobby project, the backend may be discontinued at any time, in which case stored data will be deleted.

Deleting your data

To delete your walktok profile and associated gameplay data, email neilsgr8@gmail.com from the address tied to your account (or include your display name and crew invite code so we can identify the right record). Your data will be removed within 30 days.

You can also uninstall the app at any time, which clears the locally cached copy on your device.

Children's privacy

walktok is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided data through the app, contact neilsgr8@gmail.com and the record will be removed.

Security

walktok uses HTTPS for all communication with the Supabase backend. Row-level security policies restrict access to crew data to the members of that crew. No system is perfectly secure; please do not store sensitive information in your display name or feed posts.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, the "Last updated" date above will change and, where practical, a notice will appear in the app on next launch.

Contact

Questions about this policy or requests about your data: neilsgr8@gmail.com.
